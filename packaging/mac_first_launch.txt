Film Converter for Mac
======================

Install
  Drag "Film Converter" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
  Then eject the disk image ("Film Converter" in Finder's sidebar).

First launch (only once)
  The app isn't registered with Apple (that costs $99 a year), so macOS
  blocks it the first time with a message like
  "Film Converter Not Opened" or "Apple could not verify ...".
  This is expected. To allow it:

  1. Open the app from Applications. When the warning appears, click "Done"
     (or "OK"). Do NOT click "Move to Trash".
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, and scroll down to the
     "Security" section. Next to "Film Converter was blocked ...", click
     "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  3. The app opens. From now on it opens normally.

  (On macOS 14 Sonoma you can instead right-click the app, choose "Open",
  then "Open" again.)

  If macOS asks whether Film Converter may access files in your Documents,
  Desktop or Downloads folder, click "Allow": that's where your scans are.

Requirements
  A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

Using it
  Press Cmd+O and pick any scan in a roll folder (or drag the folder onto
  the window). Help > Keyboard shortcuts lists all shortcuts.
  Your RAW files are never changed.
